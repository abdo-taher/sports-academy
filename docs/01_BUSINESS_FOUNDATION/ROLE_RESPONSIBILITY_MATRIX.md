# Canonical Role Responsibility Matrix

## Governance

This matrix applies the approved creator/reviewer separation in BD-012 and process-level authority/delegation model in BD-025. It does not create authority beyond the explicitly listed process and scope.

| Role | Confirmed Responsibilities | Participating Processes | Confirmed Actions | Approval Boundary | Notifications and Visibility | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parent / Guardian | Supply registration information, monitor linked Students, submit configured requests, interact with visible Posts/Surveys and make Payments where responsible | Registration, Trial, Subscription, Payment, Freeze, Transfer, Excuse, Renewal, Social, Session Feedback | Request, comment/react/respond where visible, view linked records, pay | Cannot modify official Attendance/Evaluation or self-approve sensitive outcomes | Limited to linked Students, scoped Guardian relationship and explicit content audience | APPROVED |
| Student | Participate in Training/Evaluation and self-manage account/consent/billing when adult | Enrollment, Attendance, Evaluation, Change, Exit | Attend, view and initiate only configured self-service actions | No implicit approval authority | Own/scoped visibility under relationship and role policy | APPROVED-WITH-CONFIGURED-ACTIONS |
| Coach | Conduct assigned Sessions, record Evaluations/recommendations, write reports and create scoped Posts/Surveys | Training, Attendance support, Evaluation, Reports, Social, Survey | Conduct, assess, recommend, report, publish/interact within assignment | Cannot approve own official Evaluation or gain Academy-wide moderation from authorship | Explicit Branch/Sport/Program/Group/Session scope | APPROVED |
| Supervisor | Review operations and official Evaluations; make configured governed decisions | Oversight, Evaluation review/publication, corrections | Review, approve/reject and monitor where assigned | Only processes assigned in authority matrix | Configured operational scope | APPROVED |
| Reception | Handle Lead, Trial booking, Guardian setup, duplicate check and enrollment execution | Acquisition, Trial, Student/Guardian setup | Create/match records and bookings; execute approved enrollment | Sensitive exceptions require configured approval | Branch enrollment scope | APPROVED |
| Accountant / Finance | Record/follow Payments, plans, debt, Discounts and compensating events | Payment, Refund/Reversal, Renewal, Finance reporting | Record, review and execute within configured financial authority | Approval limits/types follow process matrix | Branch or Academy financial scope | APPROVED-WITH-CONFIGURED-LIMITS |
| Academy Admin | Own Academy-wide/cross-Branch governance, configuration and assigned approvals | Academy/Branch states, configuration, authority, cross-Branch processes | Configure, review, approve and administer as explicitly assigned | Technical elevation does not expand business authority | Academy-wide/cross-Branch | APPROVED |
| Super Admin | Existing elevated administrative role in legacy permissions material | Platform-level administration | Only actions explicitly granted by approved permissions | Business ownership is not inferred from elevated technical access | Scope requires role-catalog approval | SUPPORTING-EVIDENCE |
| Branch Admin | Branch-scoped operational administration under BD-025 | Branch setup, Branch configuration and authorized Branch operations | Only process actions explicitly assigned within the Branch | Cannot exercise Academy-wide/cross-Branch authority unless explicitly elevated | Branch scope; delegation remains bounded | APPROVED-WITH-CONFIGURED-PROCESS-BINDINGS |

## Responsibility Rules

- Elevated access does not create business ownership or approval authority.
- Initiation, review, approval, rejection and execution are recorded separately when the process supports them.
- A role marked `PROPOSED-NOT-APPROVED` must not be provisioned from this document.
- The lifecycle files define participation; this matrix is the canonical cross-process role index.
