# Product Vision

The Sports Academy Platform manages the business operation of a sports academy: branches, sports, students, parents, coaches, groups, sessions, subscriptions, payments, attendance, evaluations, communication and reporting.

## Current Scope

Current scope is limited to business behavior confirmed in domain files and approved decisions.

## Future Scope

Events, Tournaments, Camps, related Certificates, marketplace/community activity and multi-tenant expansion are Future scope under BD-005/BD-027. Other roadmap concepts remain future unless explicitly approved later.
