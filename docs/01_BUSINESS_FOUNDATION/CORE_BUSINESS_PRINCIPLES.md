# Core Business Principles

- Business decisions govern downstream implementation.
- Business rules are canonical only when recorded in domain rules or approved decision records.
- Open questions must remain explicit.
- Generated policy values are not approved truth.
- History, audit and financial integrity must be preserved.
- Archive is not permanent deletion.
- Branch Sport availability is approved and must be enforced across registration, trial, program/service selection, group creation, scheduling, subscription, API, UX and QA.
