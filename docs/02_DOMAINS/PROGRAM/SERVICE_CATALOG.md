# Service Catalog and Offering Boundary

## Canonical Ownership

The Program domain owns Service Catalog / Offering. BD-006 approves Program as the training offering and Service / Subscription Plan as the commercial package of that offering.

## Known Facts

- Sport and Level exist.
- Branch Sport availability is governed by BD-002 and BR-BRA-001.
- A Subscription currently belongs to one Student, one Sport and one Level under BR-SUB-001.
- Historical Subscription prices are not changed retroactively under BR-FIN-004.

## Approved Distinctions

- **Sport** is the discipline.
- **Program** defines what training experience is provided and belongs to a Sport.
- **Service / Subscription Plan** defines how the Program is packaged and sold, including configured commercial terms.
- **Student Subscription** is one Student's purchased entitlement under one Plan.
- **Payment** records money and does not own Program or Subscription meaning.
- **Branch availability** is separate: Program must be explicitly enabled in the Branch.

## Affected Lifecycles

Registration/Trial selection, placement, Group setup, Subscription creation, pricing, enrollment, scheduling, transfer, renewal and reporting use the approved hierarchy and explicit Branch availability.

## Governance

No Service Catalog entry may bypass Branch Sport or Program availability. Plan configuration must preserve Program identity, historical pricing and effective dates.
