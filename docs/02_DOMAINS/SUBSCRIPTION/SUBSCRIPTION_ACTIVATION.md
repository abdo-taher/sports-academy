# Subscription Activation

Subscription activation requires financial clearance under BD-014.

Approved facts:

- BR-SUB-001 — Each Subscription belongs to one Student, one Sport and one Level.
- BR-SUB-005 — Normal training requires an eligible Subscription.
- BR-FIN-001 — A Payment record is linked to a Subscription under the current model.

Financial clearance means full approved Payment or satisfaction of the required initial conditions of an approved installment/debt plan. Manual activation outside clearance requires explicit authorized approval with reason, actor, date/time and Audit History. Exact monetary thresholds are configuration.
