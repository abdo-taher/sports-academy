# Session Feedback and Comments

## Distinctions

- **Official Evaluation:** governed assessment whose approved version may support placement or progression.
- **Session Feedback:** Student-specific or Session-level observations, configured scores, notes and assessment outcomes.
- **Comment:** append-only participant feedback attached to a visible feedback/Evaluation version.

Session Feedback and comments cannot change Level, overwrite an Official Evaluation or substitute for an approved correction.

## Authority and Visibility

An assigned authorized Coach or authorized Admin/Evaluator records Session Feedback. Every item is explicitly `Internal Staff` or `Shared with Parent/Student`; internal notes are never exposed automatically.

Authorized Coach, Admin/Supervisor and a linked Guardian may comment only where visibility allows. Parent comments are feedback, not an assessment decision. Moderation may hide a comment from normal view while preserving author, reason, moderator and history.

## Correction Boundary

Official Evaluation correction creates a new reviewed/approved version and preserves author, reviewer, approval, reason and timestamps. Existing comments remain historically attached to their original target/version.
