# Social and Survey Lifecycle

## Post Lifecycle

| From | Action | To | Authority | Required result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | Create | Draft | Academy Admin or authorized Coach in scope | Author and audience recorded |
| Draft | Publish | Published | Authorized creator/publisher | Visibility begins only for audience |
| Published | Edit | Edited | Author within policy or authorized moderator | Prior content/version retained |
| Published/Edited | Moderate/hide | Hidden/Moderated | Academy Admin or scoped moderator | Reason, actor and evidence retained |
| Any non-archived | Archive | Archived | Author if policy allows or moderator | No new interaction; history remains |

Visible authorized Students, Guardians, Coaches and Admins may react/comment where comments are enabled. Out-of-scope users are denied. Moderation hides content from normal visibility without destroying history.

## Survey Lifecycle

| From | Action | To | Authority | Required result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | Create | Draft | Academy Admin or authorized scoped Coach | Questions, audience and response policy recorded |
| Draft | Publish/open | Published/Open | Authorized creator/publisher | Open/close period and template version fixed |
| Open | Submit/edit response | Open | Authorized audience member | Response policy enforced |
| Open | Close or reach close time | Closed | Owner/authorized actor or effective time | No new submissions/edits |
| Closed | Archive | Archived | Authorized Survey owner | Results/history remain under visibility policy |

A response still in progress at closure is not accepted as submitted; it is rejected or retained only as a non-response draft if the Survey policy expressly allows draft retention.

## Communication Handoff

Publication, moderation or closure may trigger operational Notification, but delivery channel/retry mechanics do not redefine Post or Survey state.
