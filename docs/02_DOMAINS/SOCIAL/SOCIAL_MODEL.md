# Social and Survey Model

## Business Concepts

| Concept | Meaning | Not the same as |
|---|---|---|
| Post | Audience-scoped feed content authored by an authorized user. | Notification or Survey |
| Post Comment | Appendable interaction attached to a visible Post. | Official Evaluation or operational notification |
| Reaction | Attributed interaction with a visible Post. | Survey response |
| Survey | Governed container for one or more Questions. | Post or fixed Poll |
| Survey Question | Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Yes/No, configurable Rating Scale or Free Text prompt. | One universal rating schema |
| Survey Response | Governed submitted answers under anonymity, multiplicity, edit and visibility policy. | Comment |

## Relationships

Post → Author + Audience → Comments/Reactions  
Survey → Questions + Audience + Response Policy → Responses

Every object is Academy-owned and may be scoped to Branch, Sport, Program, Group or selected authorized users.
