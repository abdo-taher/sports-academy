# Parent Journey

## End-to-End Flow

| Stage | Approved Behavior | Boundary / Open Decision | History and Hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registration relationship | Minor requires at least one Guardian and exactly one Primary; multiple Guardians allowed. | Adult may self-manage; billing may be another linked Guardian. | BR-PAR-006–008; every relationship change is historical. |
| Linked Student visibility | Parent sees only linked Students and scoped information. | Primary operational contact and additional/billing access stay distinct. | BR-PAR-002/007, BR-COM-003. |
| Trial and Evaluation | Parent may request/monitor; official facts use approved roles. | Optional Trial policy; creator/reviewer Evaluation separation. | BR-TRI-004–005, BR-EVA-004–006. |
| Subscription and Payment | Authorized account owner pays or follows approved plan and requests changes. | Financial clearance/staged escalation are deterministic. | BR-SUB-008–009, BR-FIN-006. |
| Training and Attendance | View records and submit configured Excuse/correction request. | Cannot edit official Attendance/Evaluation. | BR-PAR-003, BR-ATT-004–005. |
| Freeze / Transfer / Renewal | Initiate under process authority and configured policy. | Approval/effective-date behavior follows owning lifecycle. | BR-FRZ-001–004, BR-SUB-007/010, BR-TRN-001–002. |
| Exit / Archive / Return | Relationship/access reviewed explicitly. | Nothing silently disappears or automatically restores. | BR-PAR-004–005, BR-ARC-003. |

## Exception Boundary

Duplicate registration uses authorized association/merge with provenance. A Parent comment never becomes Attendance, Evaluation, Payment or approval truth. Failed delivery does not change the underlying business outcome.

## Journey Completeness

`PARTIAL — BUSINESS GAP NBCG-008`: approved Guardian scope is usable; relationship states and pending-request authority revalidation are not defined.
