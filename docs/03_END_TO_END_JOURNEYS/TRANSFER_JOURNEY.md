# Typed Transfer Journey

## Approved Flow

| Stage | Group Transfer | Level Transfer | Sport Transfer | Branch Transfer | Shared History / Guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request | Identify compatible destination Group | Identify proposed Level | Identify new Sport/Program relationships | Identify destination Branch | Preserve type, old/proposed assignment, requester and reason |
| Validate | Capacity, Program/Sport/Level compatibility | Approved Evaluation and criteria | Sport eligibility, Branch availability and commercial impact | Destination Sport/Program enablement, capacity and operation | Failure rejects/holds with explicit reason; no current assignment changes |
| Review / Approve | Configured Group Transfer authority | Evaluation/placement decision authority | Configured Sport Transfer authority | Academy/cross-Branch Transfer authority | Process authority matrix; request/reviewer/approver separation where configured |
| Effective change | Update current Group and future Sessions | Update Level and resulting Group/future Sessions | Apply approved Program/Plan/Subscription effects | Apply Branch/Group/future Session and approved Subscription effects | Apply from effective date; historical Sessions/Attendance/Evaluation/Payments never rewritten |
| Notify / Report | Affected Student/Guardian/Coaches/Admin | Same | Same plus commercial contacts | Source/destination operations and customer | Decision, effective effects, notification and Audit Trail retained |

## Rejection and Cancellation

Rejected or cancelled Transfer preserves request, type, reason, actor and decision history and changes no assignment. An approved Transfer that becomes invalid before its effective date is held for authorized resolution; it is never partially applied.

`BUSINESS READY WITH CONFIGURATION REQUIRED` — eligibility values, capacity and exact role bindings are configured per Transfer type.
