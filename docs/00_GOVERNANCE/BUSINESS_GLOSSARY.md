# Business Glossary

Canonical Business Source of Truth uses professional English. Arabic aliases are retained only as lookup aids.

| Canonical Term | Definition | Common Alias | Arabic Alias | Related Domains | Deprecated Terms | Governance Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Academy | The organization providing sports training and operating Branches. | Academy organization | أكاديمية | Academy | Tenant | Current scope is one Academy with multiple Branches under BD-005; an independent tenant is future scope and not interchangeable. |
| Branch | An operating location belonging to an Academy. | Branch facility, Branch Manager context | فرع | Cross-domain where applicable | Branch facility, Branch Manager context | A facility inside a Branch is not the Branch itself. |
| Sport | A sports discipline offered by the Academy and explicitly enabled where available in a Branch. | Sport domain |  | Sport, Branch, Program, Subscription | Sport domain | Branch Sport availability follows BD-002/BR-BRA-001; other configuration follows BD-028. |
| Level | The finer Student progression unit owned by a Stage in a Sport/Program context; governed movement uses versioned criteria. | Skill level, Grade | مستوى | Evaluation, Sport | Stage | Level criteria are Sport/Program-specific; Level ≠ Stage and no global numeric threshold is inferred. |
| Coach | A person responsible for group or private training. | Instructor | مدرب | Coach, Group, Session, Evaluation | Instructor | Use Coach. |
| Student | The person receiving training and tracked throughout the academy lifecycle. | Athlete, Player, Trial Student | طالب / لاعب متدرب | Cross-domain where applicable | Athlete, Player, Trial Student | Athlete/Player may be descriptive but are not canonical entity names. |
| Lead | A separate pre-Student record for inquiry, Trial, follow-up and conversion tracking. | Potential Student, Prospect |  | Student | Potential Student, Prospect | Conversion preserves provenance and prevents silent duplicate Student creation. |
| Trial | An optional pre-enrollment process governed by Academy/Program policy. | Free Session, Trial Session |  | Student, Program | Free Session, Trial Session | Trial never automatically creates Student or Subscription. |
| Guardian | A person linked to a Student through an authorized care, consent or access relationship; a minor has at least one Guardian and exactly one Primary Guardian at a time. | Parent where factually applicable | ولي الأمر | Parent | Student; billing responsibility | Not every Guardian is necessarily a biological/legal Parent; access scope and billing authority are separate. |
| Primary Guardian | The one linked Guardian designated as the default operational contact for a minor at an effective time. | Primary contact | ولي الأمر الأساسي | Parent, Communication | Billing authority; sole Guardian | A minor may have multiple Guardians but exactly one Primary Guardian; billing may belong to another authorized Guardian. |
| Parent | A kinship description for a Student's parent who may also hold a governed Guardian relationship. | Guardian | والد / والدة | Parent | Primary Guardian; billing party | Parent describes the person; Guardian describes the authorized platform relationship. Do not infer access from kinship alone. |
| Training Group | A recurring training unit associated with a Sport, Level, Primary Coach and schedule. | Group | مجموعة تدريبية | Cross-domain where applicable | Group | Use Training Group on first reference; Group thereafter. |
| Schedule | The planned recurring timing for a Training Group or private training. | Timetable |  | Group, Session, Coach | Timetable | Distinct from an actual Training Session. |
| Training Session | A specific training occurrence on a date and time. | Session, Class | حصة تدريبية | Cross-domain where applicable | Session, Class | Use Training Session on first reference. |
| Attendance | The record of Student or Coach participation in a Training Session. | Check-in | حضور | Cross-domain where applicable | Check-in | Check-in is an action, not the complete Attendance record. |
| Evaluation | A recorded assessment of Student performance. | Assessment | تقييم | Cross-domain where applicable | Assessment | Initial, trial and periodic types remain distinguishable. |
| Training Plan | A planned set of training activities associated with Sport/Level/Group as documented. | Plan |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Plan | Not the same as Subscription Plan. |
| Daily Report | A report written after a Training Session by an authorized Coach or Supervisor. | Session report |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Session report | Distinct from Evaluation. |
| Subscription | The agreement granting a Student access to a defined training service for a period and session quantity. | Membership, package | اشتراك | Cross-domain where applicable | Membership, package | Subscription is not the Student and is not the same as a reusable plan. |
| Program | A Sport-owned business training offering defining what training experience the Academy provides. | Offering | برنامج | Program | Offering | Not a purchased entitlement or Payment. |
| Service | The sellable service concept exposed through a Subscription Plan for a Program; it describes the catalog offer, not a Student's purchased entitlement. | Catalog service | خدمة | Program, Subscription | Subscription | Service is not a Program and is not a Student Subscription. Where the repository says Service/Subscription Plan, the Plan is the governed catalog record. |
| Subscription Plan | The commercial Service/package through which a Program is sold. | Service Catalog item, package |  | Program, Subscription | Service Catalog item, package | A Student Subscription is the purchased instance. |
| Plan | A context-sensitive shorthand that must be qualified as Subscription Plan or Training Plan. | Package | خطة | Program, Subscription, Group | Unqualified Plan | Never infer which Plan type is meant; Subscription Plan and Training Plan are not interchangeable. |
| Session Balance | Remaining training-session entitlement owned by one Subscription. | Credits |  | Subscription, Attendance | Credits | Never negative; balances do not merge. |
| Subscription Ledger | The history of session credits, deductions, restorations and adjustments for a Subscription. | Attendance Ledger |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Attendance Ledger | Preferred term: Subscription Ledger. |
| Invoice | A statement of the amount due for Subscription services; it is not proof of Payment. | Bill |  | Payment | Bill | Financial clearance follows BR-SUB-008. |
| Payment | A recorded transfer of money against a Subscription/Invoice under the current model. | Transaction | دفعة | Cross-domain where applicable | Transaction | Financial adjustment and Payment are distinct. |
| Refund | A financial record returning an approved amount without deleting the original Payment. | Reversal |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Reversal | Reversal corrects/cancels a transaction; Refund returns money. |
| Reversal | A compensating financial record that cancels/corrects the accounting effect of an earlier transaction while retaining the original. | Void, correction | عكس قيد | Payment | Refund; deletion | Reversal does not necessarily return money and never erases the original Payment. |
| Discount | An approved reduction governed by type, eligibility, value, stacking and authority; an exceptional/manual Discount requires recorded approval. | Offer, reduction | خصم | Payment, Subscription | Price override | Discount is not a Payment, Refund or silent historical price edit. |
| Debt | An outstanding approved financial obligation associated with a Subscription/payment arrangement and governed escalation stage. | Balance due, arrears | مديونية | Payment, Subscription | Failed Payment | Debt status is not itself a Student state or automatic access consequence. |
| Subscription Freeze | An approved, time-bound pause of normal Subscription training eligibility that extends expiry by the frozen duration. | Freeze | تجميد الاشتراك | Subscription | Student inactivity; Subscription expiry | BD-019 governs effects; duration, limits and fees are configuration. |
| Freeze Request | A request to pause or change Subscription operation for a period. | Freeze | طلب تجميد الاشتراك | Subscription | Freeze | Freeze Request is the decision process; Subscription Freeze is the effect if approved. |
| Resume | The approved or scheduled end of an effective Subscription Freeze, after which normal eligibility gates are evaluated again. | Unfreeze | استئناف | Subscription | Student reactivation; Renewal | Resume does not automatically activate an otherwise expired, unpaid or ineligible Subscription and does not change Student state. |
| Transfer Request | A request to change Group, Level, Sport or Branch according to an approved transfer type. | Move, transfer | طلب نقل | Cross-domain where applicable | Move, transfer | Each transfer type requires separate effects. |
| Excuse | A reason submitted for an absence and reviewed according to policy. | Apology, absence request |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Apology, absence request | An accepted Excuse does not define the compensation outcome by itself. |
| Make-up Session | A compensatory Training Session created or granted according to an approved policy. | Compensation, Makeup Token, restored session | حصة تعويضية | Cross-domain where applicable | Compensation, Makeup Token, restored session | Session restoration, expiry extension and Make-up are not interchangeable. |
| Renewal | Creation of a new Subscription linked to the previous Subscription while preserving the prior commercial period and history. | Extension |  | Subscription, Payment | Extension | It does not overwrite/extend the old record as the same period. |
| Registration | Capture of a prospective or formal person's initial details and governed identity checks. | Sign-up | تسجيل | Student | Enrollment; Subscription activation | Registration does not by itself create training entitlement. |
| Enrollment | Formal admission that creates/establishes the Student after applicable Trial/Evaluation and before Subscription. | Registration | قيد | Student, Subscription | Registration; conversion | Enrollment is not Subscription activation and does not silently create Payment or entitlement. |
| Cancellation | A governed termination of a scheduled/requested action or record state that preserves history and applies the domain-specific outcome. | Delete | إلغاء | Session, Trial, Subscription, Transfer | Archive; deletion | Cancellation effects are not universal; use the owning lifecycle and never treat it as deletion. |
| Expiry | The effective end of a time-bounded entitlement, assignment, document or policy validity period. | Deactivation | انتهاء | Subscription, Documents, Coach | Archive; cancellation | Subscription expiry creates no implicit attendance grace and does not automatically Archive the Student. |
| Capacity | The configured permitted occupancy or workload boundary for a Group, Session or relevant resource. | Seats, limit | سعة | Group, Session, Transfer | Availability | Capacity is not proof of suitability, entitlement or Branch/Program availability. |
| Business Timeline | The business-facing chronological history of significant events for an entity. | Timeline |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Activity Log | Business Timeline is not the same as a technical Audit Log or raw Activity Log. |
| Activity Log | A record of system/user activity events that may support operations but is not automatically business history or approval evidence. | Activity feed |  | Academy / technical downstream | Business Timeline; Audit Log | Activity Log ≠ Audit Log and cannot prove business authority by itself. |
| Audit Log | A controlled record of who changed what, when and why. | Audit trail |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Activity Log | Audit Log is evidence of governed action; technical storage is downstream. |
| Decision History | The retained sequence of requests, reviews, approvals, rejections, overrides, reasons and effective outcomes for a governed decision. | Approval history |  | Academy and owning process | Audit Log; Business Timeline | Decision History records business deliberation/outcome; Audit Log records controlled changes. |
| Recommendation | A proposed assessment, option or advised outcome that has not become approved Business Truth merely by being recorded. | Suggestion | توصية | Evaluation / Governance | Approved Decision | Recommendation ≠ approved Decision; only the governed decision authority can approve the final outcome. |
| Notification | A one-way business communication generated for a purpose and recipient. | Alert |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Alert | Distinct from a conversation Message. |
| Message | A communication exchanged between authorized parties. | Chat |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Chat | Permissions and retention are policy-dependent. |
| Event | A planned academy activity outside ordinary recurring training. | Tournament, Camp |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Tournament, Camp | Tournament and Camp are possible event types, currently Future. |
| Facility | A physical space or resource inside or associated with a Branch, such as a court or room. | Venue | منشأة | Branch, Session | Branch | Facility is not the Branch. A complete current Facility lifecycle is not separately approved. |
| Archive | A non-destructive state preserving historical information outside normal operation. | Delete | أرشفة | Cross-domain where applicable | Delete | Archive is not permanent deletion. |
| Restore | A fresh authorized review/action that returns an Archived record to an approved operational state without silently restoring old relationships or entitlement. | Reactivate, Return | استعادة | Student and owning domain | Unarchive; automatic reactivation | Restore is not automatic reactivation of Subscription, Group, account or prior balance. |
| Configuration | An effective-dated governed value for an already-approved variable policy dimension, resolved by declared scope and precedence. | Setting | إعداد | Academy / owning domain | Business Decision; invariant | Configuration supplies values; it cannot invent missing behavior or override a non-configurable invariant. |
| Policy | An approved business constraint or variable decision dimension that governs behavior and may expose named configuration items. | Rule set | سياسة | Owning business domain | Configuration value; technical setting | Policy defines allowed behavior; Configuration supplies approved variable values. |
| Authority | The explicit business right, within process and scope, to initiate, review, approve, reject or execute an action. | Business permission | سلطة | Academy and owning process | Technical access; role name | Possessing a Role or technical permission alone does not prove business authority. |
| Delegation | A recorded, revocable, time- and scope-bound grant of existing business authority that never exceeds the delegator's authority. | Temporary authority | تفويض | Academy | Permanent role assignment; impersonation | Delegation does not permanently change role and expires according to its recorded boundary. |
| Academy Admin | An administrative role operating the Academy. | Admin, Academy Manager |  | Cross-domain where applicable | Admin, Academy Manager | Generic Admin should be replaced by the exact role when known. |
