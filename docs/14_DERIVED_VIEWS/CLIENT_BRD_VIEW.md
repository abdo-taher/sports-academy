## Approved Operating Model

- The Academy owns defaults; an approved more-specific override may apply by declared scope and effective date.
- Branch Sport availability is explicit and cannot be bypassed by a generic override.
- Minors require at least one Guardian and exactly one Primary Guardian; billing responsibility is separate.
- Students may hold multiple active Subscriptions/Groups, while every Attendance uses exactly one eligible Subscription.
- Freeze, Renewal, typed Transfer, Archive and Restore have independent, audited lifecycles.
- Process-level authority, bounded delegation and history apply to sensitive operations.
