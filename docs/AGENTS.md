# Documentation Editing Instructions

Documentation changes must follow `00_GOVERNANCE/AI_CHANGE_PROPAGATION_PROTOCOL.md`.

Business documentation authority order:

1. approved current Business Decisions and Changes;
2. canonical Business Rules;
3. canonical Domain models and lifecycles;
4. Policies and configuration;
5. End-to-End Journeys;
6. Requirements;
7. technical specifications;
8. Derived Views;
9. historical evidence.

Never introduce Business Truth from technical documentation. Preserve Rule, Decision, Requirement and downstream traceability. Update related indexes, client presentation and the cumulative Change Log when impacted. Historical material is never current authority.
